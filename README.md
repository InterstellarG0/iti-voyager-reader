ITI Voyager Reader 🚀

Explore Knowledge. Expand Horizons.

ITI Voyager Reader is a reading and knowledge exploration project under **Interstellar Technologies Initiative, LLC (ITI)**. Its mission is to support digital learning, research, and discovery through accessible technology.

🌌 Vision

We believe access to knowledge is fundamental to human progress. Voyager Reader is part of ITI’s broader vision to build tools that empower individuals to learn, explore ideas, and participate in a more connected, knowledge-driven future.

🎯 Objectives

- Encourage lifelong learning and digital literacy.
- Explore accessible, user-friendly reading experiences.
- Support independent research and knowledge discovery.
- Contribute to ITI’s growing ecosystem of educational and exploration-focused technology.
